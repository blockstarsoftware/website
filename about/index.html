About Blockstar

My grandfather owned liquor stores in old South Side Chicago called Block-Starr Liquors. I never met him, but I loved his logo, and Blockstar's is inspired by it.

Human vision, AI execution

All of my software is made with AI. I've been a Mac power user my whole life and spent six years as an Apple Genius, but I never learned to write code. I always had ideas for software I wanted to make, and now I can.

My software revolves around my passions. I'm delighted to share what I'm building, and I hope some of it is useful to you.

Made on a Mac for the Mac

Every app is written in Swift and native to the Mac. No Electron here. All of it is free and open source.

Questions and ideas are welcome on each project's GitHub page.
