BitPolish

One switch for bit-perfect listening. Your external DAC follows each Apple Music track's sample rate, at the bit depth you choose.

Download Source

Coming soon · Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later on Apple Silicon · Free, MIT License

BitPolish lives in your menu bar. Turn on Bit-Perfect Listening for a session, and it keeps your external DAC at each track's own sample rate as Apple Music plays your local library, at a locked bit depth: 24-bit by default, or the device's maximum. Turn it off, and your DAC goes back to the format it had before.

Careful with your gear

  • Every change to your audio device has a restore point.
  • Nothing moves sound to your speakers without a click from you.
  • No permission prompts.
  • A small mark in the menu bar tells you when playback isn't bit-perfect.

BitPolish will be a free download, signed and notarized by Apple, with its source on GitHub.
