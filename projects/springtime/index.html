SpringtimeNative Mac app

If you love spring-loaded
folders in Finder,
you'll love Springtime.

One menu bar item gives you immediate access to every Finder window on every Space, any folder from your home folder, the apps that can open your file, and a shelf to park it for later.

In development · Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later · Free

Why it exists

FB24754909

For those who have the FB24754909 blues, or those who simply wanted a new tool.

While macOS 27 has brought its share of refreshing refinements, it has unfortunately introduced other regressions. Two of them broke things I do all day.

Dragging to a Finder window on another Space. Open a Finder window on one Space, then switch to another. From the new Space, drag a file onto Finder in the Dock and hold it there. You're presented with the presently open Finder windows, including the one from your first Space. If you drag your file there, macOS used to take you to that Finder window so you could drop the file in. Now it fails, leaving you where you are, and the drag has nowhere to go.

Spring-loaded folders for files dragged out of apps. With a Finder window open, drag a file out of another application, such as an attachment in Mail, and drag it over the Finder icon in the Dock. Again, you're presented with a display of open Finder windows, but it fails in the same way.

Stage Manager could have filled in, but it's too buggy. I reported it to Apple as FB24754909.

This is what prompted me to create this little utility, which on some level does the job better than a working Finder would have, but I'd prefer not to need it.

How it works

Drag, rest, choose

Features

Where it goes

Browse from Home

Folders open in columns as you rest on them, so you can drill down as far as you like and drop.

Finder windows

Every open Finder window, on every Space, with a picture of each. Rest on one and Springtime takes you to it, file still in hand. Or, if you'd rather stay put, it draws a live copy of the window right where you are.

Apps

The running apps that can open the file, with their windows. Drop to open it there, or carry it to the exact spot in a window.

Park

Set the file on a shelf and come back for it later.

It follows Finder's rules: a move on the same disk, a copy to another, Option to copy, Command to move. It asks before replacing a file with the same name, and Undo takes back your last drops. Attachments dragged out of Mail arrive as the real files.
