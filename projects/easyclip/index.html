EasyClip

Cut exactly the part of a video you want, to the millisecond, without changing the video. Or join several clips into a highlight reel.

Download Source

In development · Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later and ffmpeg · Free, MIT License

Drop a video on EasyClip, set the start and end, and export. The video isn't re-encoded, so the clip looks exactly like the original.

What it does

  • Trim points accurate to the millisecond, typed as HH:MM:SS:MS or set by dragging.
  • A transcript of the video, made with MacWhisper, that you can search. Select a phrase and EasyClip suggests a clip from its words.
  • Highlight reels: several named clips from one video, in any order, with optional text cards and fades, exported as one file.
  • Audio normalization, on by default, and an optional extra version small enough to send in Signal.
