Their initiatives

Unconditional Freedom serves people society has given up on, in prison, in addiction and without a home. It asks what is possible for them, not what is wrong with them. These are the initiatives:

Free Food

Restaurant-quality three-course meals, with cloth napkins and fresh flowers, for anyone who walks in.

Prison Monastery

Contemplative practice that turns prisons into places of reflection and healing.

Underground Railroad

Support for people leaving prison, and their families, as they build a new life.

Free the Artist

A permanent gallery and living archive for incarcerated artists.
